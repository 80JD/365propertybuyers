We Buy Houses in Sherwood, Arkansas

Sherwood is mostly brick ranch houses from the 1970s through the 90s on quiet streets off Kiehl Avenue and Brockington Road, with newer subdivisions filling in toward the north. It's the kind of housing stock that was built well and then, in a lot of cases, left alone for thirty years. That's exactly what we buy.

We're 15 minutes away in Cabot, family-owned and veteran-owned. If the Sherwood house needs a roof, a kitchen and a new HVAC before a retail buyer would touch it, we'll buy it today as it sits and handle that work ourselves.

Where Sherwood is, and where we are. Sherwood is in Pulaski County, part of the Little Rock–North Little Rock–Conway metro area that most people here just call Central Arkansas. It's about 15 minutes from our Cabot office. We buy throughout Sherwood — the neighborhoods along Kiehl Avenue, Brockington Road and Maryland Avenue, the Indianhead Lake area, and the newer subdivisions toward Jacksonville — and in the surrounding county. ZIP codes we buy in: 72120 and 72124.
