Privacy & website analytics

Updated November 4, 2026

Information you send us

When you submit a property inquiry, we receive the contact and property information you provide so we can respond. Website forms currently use Formspree to deliver inquiries. Please do not enter Social Security numbers, bank details or other sensitive documents into website forms.

Website traffic measurement

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages people visit and whether visits come from places such as our Google Business Profile. Google Analytics uses cookies and receives browser and device information, page visits and traffic-source information. We do not send your inquiry form contents, name, email, phone number or property address as analytics fields. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled in our website tag.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Learn about Formspree's privacy practices.

Your analytics choice

You can turn off analytics for this browser. This choice does not affect submitting an inquiry. The website also respects the browser's Do Not Track setting. Your preference is saved in this browser's local storage; clearing browser storage resets it.

For privacy questions, email joey@365propertybuyers.com.
