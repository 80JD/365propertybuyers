We Buy Houses in Lonoke, Arkansas

Lonoke is the county seat and sits on I-40 in the middle of rice and farm country. The houses here are different from the subdivisions up in Cabot: older homes near downtown, farmhouses on a few acres, manufactured homes on land, and properties that have been in one family for generations. We buy all of those.

We're 15 minutes away in Cabot, in the same county, and we're used to the things that come with Lonoke properties — well and septic, a shop building, an acre or ten, a house that's been empty since a grandparent passed. We buy as-is, pay cash, and close through a local title company.

Where Lonoke is, and where we are. Lonoke is in Lonoke County, part of the Little Rock–North Little Rock–Conway metro area that most people here just call Central Arkansas. It's the county seat. It's about 15 minutes from our Cabot office. We buy throughout Lonoke — downtown Lonoke, the neighborhoods along Highway 70 and Highway 31, the I-40 corridor, and the rural addresses between Lonoke, Cabot, Carlisle and England — and in the surrounding county.
