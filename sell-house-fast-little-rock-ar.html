We've looked at all four kinds, and we price each one on what it is, not on a zip-code average.

Situations we see most in Little Rock

Tired landlord

You own a rental in Southwest Little Rock or off Asher Avenue, the tenants have moved out (or won't), and you'd rather have a check than another turnover. We buy occupied and vacant rentals.

Inherited house in an older neighborhood

A parent's home in Capitol View, Stifft Station or the Heights that hasn't been updated since the 80s. We buy it as it sits — furniture, roof, wiring and all — and you don't drive back to Little Rock to clean it out.

Behind on the mortgage

If you're getting letters from the lender, a cash closing in a couple of weeks can stop a foreclosure before it hits your credit. Call before the sale date is set; after that the options shrink.
