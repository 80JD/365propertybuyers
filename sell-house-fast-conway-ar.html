We Buy Houses in Conway, Arkansas

Conway is the Faulkner County seat and home to three colleges — the University of Central Arkansas, Hendrix College and Central Baptist College — which means a lot of rental houses, a lot of people moving in and out, and a steady stream of older homes near downtown that need more than a coat of paint. We buy all of that, as-is, for cash.

We're a veteran-owned family buyer based in Cabot, about 45 minutes around I-40. We're not a franchise and we don't run a call center; you'll talk to Joey, we'll walk the house, and we'll close through a Faulkner County title company on the date you choose.

Where Conway is, and where we are. Conway is in Faulkner County, part of the Little Rock–North Little Rock–Conway metro area that most people here just call Central Arkansas. It's the county seat. It's about 45 minutes from our Cabot office via I-40. We buy throughout Conway — the neighborhoods around UCA and Hendrix, Old Conway and downtown, the Dave Ward Drive and Prince Street corridors, and the subdivisions toward Greenbrier, Vilonia and Mayflower — and in the surrounding county. ZIP codes we buy in: 72032, 72034 and 72035.
